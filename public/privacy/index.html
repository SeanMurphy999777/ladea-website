Privacy policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This page explains what happens to the information you send us through the contact form on ladea.ca. It is short because the site collects very little.

Who we are

Ladea Technologies is based in Montréal, Québec. For any question about your personal information, write to info@ladea.ca. That address also reaches the person responsible for the protection of personal information at Ladea Technologies.

What the contact form collects

  • your name
  • your company name
  • your email address
  • your phone number, only if you choose to give it
  • your message

The form also records which language version of the site you used, so we can reply in the same language.

Why we collect it

We use this information to respond to your inquiry and, if you want to continue the conversation, to follow up with you about it. We don’t use it for anything else.

No marketing lists

We do not add you to a newsletter or marketing list. If we ever offer one, you will only be added if you explicitly opt in.

Who has access

We don’t sell or rent your information. Your message reaches us through the tools we use to receive and manage inquiries, such as form-processing and email services. Those providers handle it on our behalf, and some of them may store data outside Québec.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiries only as long as we need them to respond and to follow up with you. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. Write to info@ladea.ca.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and it does not load third-party scripts or fonts. As with any website, our hosting provider may process technical data, such as IP addresses, to deliver and protect the site.

Changes to this page

If our practices change, we will update this page and the date at the top.
